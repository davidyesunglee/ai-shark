# The assessment AI is Gemini (not OpenAI)

The business-plan assessment is generated by **Google Gemini** — `server/openai.ts`
imports `@google/genai` (`GoogleGenAI`) and calls model `gemini-2.5-flash`. The
filename `openai.ts` and parts of `replit.md` (and the presence of `OPENAI_API_KEY`
in missing-secrets / a `javascript_openai` integration) are misleading leftovers.

**Why it matters:** public copy that names the AI vendor must say "Gemini" to stay
truthful (e.g. the FAQ answer "AI Shark uses Gemini AI…"). Don't trust the filename
or scattered "OpenAI" mentions — verify the actual client import before writing any
vendor claim.

**How to apply:** before referencing the AI provider anywhere user-facing, confirm
against the import/model call in `server/openai.ts`.
